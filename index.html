FAQ

Questions clients usually ask first.

How much does a website cost?

Small business websites start at $1,500. The final price depends on the number of pages, integrations (booking, forms, email automation), and whether you need extras like a second language. After a free scoping call you get a fixed quote, so there are no surprise invoices.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A focused small business site moves much faster than a 96-page bilingual build. Your quote includes a timeline, and I'll tell you upfront what I need from you to stick to it.

You're not based in the US. How does communication work?

I work full US Eastern business hours, 9am to 5pm, so we're online at the same time. We'll have scheduled video calls at key milestones and regular written updates in between. All work is in English.

WordPress or Webflow? Which should I choose?

I build on both. WordPress (with Bricks or Elementor) is usually best when you need flexibility and plugins. Webflow is great for design-heavy sites your team edits visually. I'll recommend one based on who updates the site and how.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. Every site is built on a CMS, and I'll walk you through editing pages, posts and services before handoff.

Is SEO included?

Technical and on-page SEO are included in every build: meta tags, schema markup, XML sitemaps, Google Search Console setup and speed optimization. Ongoing content or ranking campaigns can be quoted separately.
